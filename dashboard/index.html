About DeepGuard AI

DeepGuard AI is a production-grade, multi-modal deepfake and AI-generated content detection system built on a Layered Defense-in-Depth Architecture. It combines cryptographic provenance verification, classical forensic analysis, deep-learning detectors, and cross-modal consistency checks — fused through a Bayesian evidence engine into a 4-tier verdict that always includes an Indeterminate class to prevent false-positive accusations.

Detection Architecture (v3)

Image Detection C2PA/EXIF provenance · 2D DFT 1/f spectral decay · Wavelet-denoised noise residual (PRNU) · ELA splice detection · Multi-crop HuggingFace classifier · Per-face blend-boundary & noise-mismatch analysis
Video Detection Scene-cut keyframe selection · Landmark velocity & acceleration kinematics (3σ spike detection) · Farneback optical flow warp residual · Face identity cosine-distance tracking · Blink-rate analysis · Audio-visual lip-sync cross-correlation (MAR vs energy envelope)
Audio Detection 16 kHz mono + RMS silence trimming · Static + Δ + ΔΔ MFCC · Spectral centroid & flatness · HNR via HPSS · F₀ pitch jitter (pyin) · Phase coherence · Quality-weighted segment aggregation · Wav2Vec2-based spoof classifier
Text Detection GPT-2 perplexity + burstiness B=(σ−μ)/(σ+μ) · DetectGPT log-probability curvature · TTR / Hapax Legomena / Shannon entropy · KGW watermark z-score (multi-seed) · Formulaic AI discourse marker density
Fusion Engine Quality-aware modality gating (α_m) · Bayesian log-likelihood ratio fusion · 4-tier verdict: Verified Provenance / Likely Authentic / Indeterminate / Likely Synthetic · Cost-sensitive thresholds (τ* = C_FP / C_FP + C_FN)
Query Assistant YOLOv8 object detection on uploaded images · Gemini 2.0 Flash for open-ended forensic Q&A

Key Forensic Capabilities

  • SHA-256 chain-of-custody hash on every uploaded file
  • C2PA / JUMBF content credentials detection
  • Localized bounding-box evidence (ELA splice regions, per-face findings)
  • Temporal suspicious-interval localization in video [start_sec, end_sec]
  • Suspicious audio segment reporting with per-segment SNR quality weights
  • Suspicious text span highlighting with explanatory justifications
  • Multi-modal cross-evidence fusion via /api/detect/fusion
  • Indeterminate tier prevents false-positive accusations on low-confidence evidence
  • All verdicts include a full ranked evidence trail, not just a score

Research Foundation

Architecture grounded in the 2025–2026 forensic research consensus: no single detector is foolproof. Unimodal classifiers trained on isolated benchmarks fail under out-of-distribution conditions, social media compression, paraphrasing, and adversarial evasion (up to 80–88% attack success rate). Robustness requires layered, redundant, independently-operating forensic signals fused with calibrated uncertainty. DeepGuard AI reports evidence, not verdicts — high-impact decisions always require human review.
